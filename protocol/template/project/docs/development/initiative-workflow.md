# Initiative workflow

Follow the [workflow specification](../../evidence/authoritative/LLM/08_Codex_Initiative_Workflow_Specification.md)
and [numbered prompt library](../../template/prompts/README.md).
The initiative type, governing revision and owning stage determine the output.

## Prepare and specify

Stage 0 assesses realization fit, preserves exact identity and records lawful
execution placement. Stages 1 and 2 create and align the proposal. Stage 3
completes the initial initiative suite from
[the common sources](../../initiatives/README.md); Stage 4 aligns it.
Recover missing documentary prerequisites only through their owning activities
with actual evidence and gates.

## Decide and accept

Stages 5 and 6 record individual human decisions unless exact governing
authority supplies another bounded method. Stage 7 requires separate human
acceptance. A copied template, resolved question or passing check is not
acceptance.

## Deliver and finalize

Implementation follows stages 8 through 10 when applicable. Design uses
Stage 11; planning uses Stage 12. Stage 9 readiness is ordinarily a response
shape, not a required durable file. Preserve actual verification and all
unfinished work. Stage 13 finalizes only a supported completion claim.
Scope closure remains separate.

Use .template.md sources only at their declared destinations and triggers;
remove .template from actual record names and recalculate links.
Keep existing IDs, customized work, accepted baselines and history. Reopening,
changed claims, remote actions and branch deletion retain their governing
authority. Installing a baseline performs none of these events.
