## Purpose and standing

Record incompatibilities between proposed, planned, partially delivered, and
completed work, including effort in every open initiative. An entry records a
conflict; it does not accept a proposal, promote evidence, authorize changes,
or resolve a human decision. Apply the
[initiative coherence specification]({{relative_coherence_specification_path}}).

## Recording and resolution

- Allocate a stable `WORK-CONFLICT-NNN` ID. Link both exact work claims and
  their revisions; preserve their original authority and lifecycle standing.
  Add a matching `### WORK-CONFLICT-NNN` detail heading below the table so
  completion reviews can link its `#work-conflict-NNN` anchor. Preserve the
  full claims, evidence, blocking scope, and resolution history there.
- Describe proposed, planned, partial, and verified effort on both sides and
  the effect on scope, dependencies, compatibility, recovery, and verification.
- Name the accountable owner and precise blocked work. Stop affected acceptance,
  execution, or completion while incompatible; independent coherent work may
  continue.
- Use status `Open` or `Resolved`. Keep an open entry's decision owner,
  next action, and re-entry condition visible. Resolve only with linked
  correction or explicit human decision evidence, authority, and reconciled
  affected records; retain the original conflict and its history.
- Link each entry from affected initiative records and completion reviews.
  If one target is closed, record the finding here without refining that
  target until reopening under adopted bounded intake delegation or separate
  human authority is recorded. A separately governed
  successor may preserve and cite the closed claim.
- Also register contradictions with an invariant in the authoritative
  invariant conflict register. This work register cannot waive an invariant.
- Neither newer completion nor sunk effort outranks authority. Do not silently
  discard open work, rewrite closed claims, or close questions to manufacture
  coherence.
