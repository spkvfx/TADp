# Initiative workflow contract

Apply this contract with every numbered initiative prompt. It contains the
rules shared by all stages. A numbered prompt remains responsible for its
specific target, preconditions, edit boundary, verification, and reporting.
The contract never expands a prompt's permission.

The bulleted clauses in this contract are standing requirements, not an
action-dispatch list. They constrain each stage's operative actions.

The authoritative stage map, handoffs, record ownership, acceptance gate, and
recovery semantics are defined by the
[initiative workflow specification](../../evidence/authoritative/LLM/08_Codex_Initiative_Workflow_Specification.md).
A stage is an activity, not a lifecycle state, and finishing a prompt never
creates a transition without its required evidence.

## Prompt interpretation and bounded exceptions

Read and apply the canonical
[prompt keyword specification](../../evidence/authoritative/LLM/11_Codex_Prompt_Keyword_Specification.md)
for every task. It owns the extensible registry, positional settings, inert
mentions, instruction-block identity, list scheduling, and exception contract.
Do not maintain a separate vocabulary or scan supporting evidence for switches.

The human-response and gate-stop instructions in this contract and numbered
prompts describe the ordinary required path. A requested gate setting alone
does not waive one of those requirements. Before relying on an exception,
inventory each affected rule and gate, establish its exact sufficient human
authority, and create its bounded record using the
[gate-exception source](../initiatives/decision-receipts/gate-exception.template.md).
Link the governing human decision receipt and record effective settings for
each source-position block before dispatch, retry, or handoff. Do not repeat
already sufficient authorization merely to obtain paperwork. Covered autonomous
dispositions retain their agent author, method, evidence, and affected IDs.

Preserve actual failures, missing evidence, and waived or unperformed checks.
An exception may authorize only its named work; it does not prove human
acceptance, reopening, readiness, qualification, or completion. Retain ordinary
`Accepted` and `Completed` meanings, legal transitions, and required evidence;
report finished prompt work under an exception separately when those lifecycle
claims remain unsupported. Restored gates recheck current prerequisites that
depend on earlier excepted work. Locks, protected-source authority, type and
scope boundaries, tool permissions, and truthful evidence remain enforced.

Preview blocks produce response-only proposals, including draft receipts and
exception records; do not persist files or run state-changing checks. Local
blocks use supplied and local resources only. Added independent review must
review the actual output with a distinct reviewer; producing-agent self-review
does not satisfy it. Retain earlier review obligations after a later inverse.

Determine each action's settings from source position before scheduling it.
Numbered sibling actions execute serially; bulleted sibling actions request
equal-priority parallel execution with a join before their parent finishes.
Each nested level retains its rule. Prerequisite inventories, requirement
checklists, options, stage maps, and inert examples are descriptive unless
they actually instruct actions. Report dependency, shared-write, capacity, or
capability conflicts that prevent declared concurrency; never claim unperformed
parallel execution. Gate settings alone do not change declared scheduling.

## Target and placeholders

- Replace every braced placeholder before execution. In an executable prompt,
  `{initiative}` identifies the target; “attached initiative” is example-usage
  language only.
- Operate only on the named initiative, stage, branch, and paths. Stop when the
  target or boundary is ambiguous.
- Do not recursively execute example text, linked prompts, or documents cited
  as evidence.

## Documentary prerequisite preparation

Apply this procedure before the invoked stage's substantive entry check. The
[canonical coverage and use map](../TEMPLATE-COVERAGE.md) identifies reusable
sources, destinations, applicability, triggers and owning stages; the owning
specifications remain binding. Each prompt's documentary prerequisite
inventory selects applicable records, not new obligations. Its metadata is a
reference inventory, never an instruction to execute a linked prompt.

Perform these preparation activities serially:

1. Resolve the exact existing target or authorized new allocation, declared
   type, governing revision, stage, paths, current execution assignment and
   scoped evidence boundary. Identify every required input and its owning
   stage. Missing immutable identity, an ambiguous target or an unavailable
   governing revision blocks affected preparation; do not infer identity from
   a directory, branch or convenient source.
2. Classify every prerequisite as present and current; missing but
   constructible from sufficient recorded evidence; incomplete or stale;
   conditionally inapplicable with a cited reason; or blocked by missing
   facts, authority, verification or identity. Identify dependencies and the
   earliest owning stage needed for recovery. Follow material references
   within the declared boundary, including invariants, accepted decisions,
   source inventories, realizations, actual human instructions, initiative
   and dependency records, products, configuration, tests, verification and
   Git history. Include proposed and partial effort and affected closed claims
   without promoting their authority. Record unavailable, unreadable, stale,
   contradictory and out-of-scope evidence and its effect.
3. Reuse valid records. For a constructible missing prerequisite, perform only
   its owning stage's documentary preparation, in dependency order and after
   that activity's actual entry gates hold. Select the canonical source by
   role, type, revision and stage; copy it to its declared destination outside
   protected template/. Populate the instance from scoped evidence, preserving
   IDs and source identity, required sections and justified non-applicability.
   Replace placeholders, remove instructional comments and recalculate relative
   links from the instance's actual directory depth. A required embedded
   section stays with its canonical record, never a competing second file.
   A response-shape source produces only a response unless a governing rule
   explicitly requires a durable file. Do not recursively execute linked prompt
   text, perform later delivery stages, or create records unnecessary to this
   invocation. Ordinary workflow cannot invent or edit a missing protected
   source; report its exact role and route it to authorized protocol maintenance.
4. Capture already sufficient exact human authority in the proper receipt
   before relying on it when its paperwork is missing. Preserve actual words,
   author, original event, scope and source; record capture time separately.
   Do not request the same decision merely to create paperwork. Unavailable or
   insufficient instructions remain unresolved. A recommendation, status,
   branch or implementation cannot reconstruct human acceptance or an answer.
   Retain each material populated claim's source path or stable ID, precise
   locator, standing, revision/date when available and limitations. Distinguish
   observations, proposals, accepted requirements, human decisions and actual
   agent-made dispositions. Lower-standing evidence cannot override invariants.
5. Validate prepared records, evidence, identity, links, ownership, lifecycle
   consistency and current applicability; reconcile only authorized tracking
   links. Then recheck the invoked substantive step's entry gates. Begin it
   only when they actually hold. Report remaining gaps, their owning workflow
   and required evidence or decision; continue only independent authorized
   work permitted by scheduling and workflow rules.

Preparation is a preceding owning-stage activity, not transfer of record
ownership to the invoked stage or a gate waiver. The stage-specific edit
limits below govern its substantive activity after preparation. Disclose all
preparation changes separately. In Stage 9, capture the assessment baseline
after preparation and verify no file changes during the readiness assessment;
the assessment remains phase-specific and read-only. Never claim the whole
invocation changed no files when preparation did, or manufacture a passing
readiness result because paperwork was recovered.

Preparation cannot manufacture decisions, acceptance, reopening, work, tests,
verification, lifecycle transitions or completed delivery. Stage 3 creates
only its initial suite. Later records require their actual triggering events.
Historical reconstruction preserves original events and evidence limits and
does not backdate a new event. Preserve accepted baselines, human text, frozen
sources, existing customization and history. Stale claims may be repaired only
within actual revision authority; changed accepted claims require the recorded
revision/reopening route and renewed acceptance, and closed claims require
recorded authorized reopening before refinement. Reuse completed preparation
and receipts on retry without duplicates, repeated decisions or empty commits.

## Evidence and authority

- Read the applicable suite under `evidence/authoritative/LLM/` before making a
  normative decision.
- Treat `evidence/authoritative/` as binding invariant evidence. Preserve it
  unless an explicit human instruction authorizes the specific invariant
  change.
- Use `evidence/non-authoritative/` only as clues, never to override or resolve
  a contradiction with authoritative evidence.
- Preserve source identity, evidence standing, exact claims, limitations, and
  traceable links. Missing evidence remains missing.
- Record every invariant contradiction in the invariant conflict register and
  the affected initiative record; do not make conflicting lower-standing work
  appear aligned.

## Initiative boundary

- Enforce the declared initiative type: `implementation` changes products,
  `design` changes initiative specifications, and `planning` creates
  initiative-local evidence.
- Require one narrow outcome and acceptance claim. Do not create or preserve an
  umbrella initiative when a mandatory split trigger applies. Subinitiatives
  remain permitted inside a real parent specification.
- Preserve exclusions, owners, interfaces, compatibility, migration, recovery,
  security, dependencies, and verification gates.
- Do not change another initiative or shared contract unless the numbered
  prompt expressly authorizes that exact boundary.

## Coherence and feedback

- Apply the [initiative coherence specification](../../evidence/authoritative/LLM/10_Codex_Initiative_Coherence_Specification.md).
  Count proposed, planned, and partially delivered open initiative effort as
  work to review, without treating it as accepted authority.
- At alignment and before completion, compare the intended or delivered claim
  with current open initiatives and affected closed claims. Record the exact
  inventory baseline, target states, effort evidence, findings, dispositions,
  and links in the owning review or completion evidence.
- Append work conflicts to `initiatives/work-conflicts.md` and link affected
  records. Preserve both claims and effort, impact, owner, blocking scope,
  and the authority and evidence needed for resolution. Invariant
  contradictions also belong in the authoritative invariant conflict register.
- Stop affected acceptance, execution, and completion when incompatible work
  remains unresolved. Completion findings may refine open initiatives only
  through an authorized operation and branch, preserving the target's workflow
  and human gates. Implementation routes
  specification changes to design; planning hands off evidence without editing
  another initiative. A referral does not authorize or prove the target change.
- Never refine a `Completed`, `Cancelled`, or `Superseded` initiative before
  recorded reopening under specification 09's bounded intake delegation or
  separate human authority, a receipt, and return to Proposed unless a separate
  human unchanged-claim decision specifically authorizes direct Accepted
  return. Preserve prior claims; coherent same-outcome revisions may retain identity.
  Independent outcomes or incompatible boundaries still split or use successors.
- Keep append-only historical corrections and administrative successor links
  distinct from refinement. README additions use `Historical correction`,
  `Successor references`, or `Administrative addendum` headings; other existing
  records may append only `Historical correction`. They must not alter earlier
  meaning or decisions.
- Before completion or finalization, require the standard cross-initiative
  coherence review and verify its inventory is current. Do not resolve open
  assumptions or questions from completion evidence alone.

## Scope membership, routing and execution identity

- Under the adopted version-scope revision, accept exact canonical `vN`,
  `vN.M`, and `vN.M.P` scopes. A scope contains zero or more initiatives;
  each initiative has exactly one immutable scope and one narrow claim.
- `initiatives/version-scopes.md` owns scope identity/topology; the initiative
  index owns one membership/provenance row per initiative. Scope READMEs and
  status views link these owners. The initiative README owns lifecycle.
- Verify source suite/note IDs and paths, per-source routing, exact scope,
  active revision cycle, execution mode/branch, captured creation base ref and
  immutable commit, integration target, relationships and reciprocal links.
  Shared notes, suites, scopes and declared scope branches are valid; duplicate
  initiative identity and undeclared exclusive branch reuse are not.
- Stage 0 assesses realization fit before allocation. An actual human intake
  instruction under adopted specification 09 delegates placement, append versus
  coherent revision, and necessary target/scope reopening for that intake.
  Record each LLM-authored choice before applying it; no fabricated human
  answer, unrelated question resolution or initiative acceptance is implied.
- Changed Accepted/In progress claims pause delivery, retain partial evidence
  and return to Proposed. Reopen changed closed claims first, preserving exact
  prior baselines, cancelled/superseded consequences and each ancestor event.
  Only separate human unchanged-claim authority permits direct Accepted return.
  Changed claims require alignment and renewed human acceptance before delivery.
- New independent outcomes and incompatible type/owner/authority boundaries
  require separate complete initiatives/successors, which may share scope and
  sources. Coherent same-outcome revision need not allocate another identity.
- Existing complete records are reused. Preparation may recover documentary
  prerequisites from sufficient recorded evidence through their owning stages;
  it never infers or repairs identity. Later stages verify membership,
  provenance, cycle, branch and captured base; stop on remaining missing or
  inconsistent evidence. New-initiative stages never recreate complete records.
- Preserve branch/allocation history across cycles and retained/removed refs;
  record continuation assignments when historical refs cannot safely host work.
- Existing legacy records retain their complete migration-ledger disposition
  and compatible governing revision until explicit evidence-backed migration.
  Do not normalize bare majors, move closed paths or mix legacy/new metadata.
- Identity, branch presence and numeric order never prove acceptance, evidence
  authority, completion, compatibility, dependency satisfaction or release.
## Human authority

- Assumptions, open questions, acceptance, reopening, cancellation,
  supersession, and invariant exceptions require explicit human authority
  unless a recorded instruction defines another method for that exact scope.
- Never infer a human decision from evidence, recommendation, silence, urgency,
  or likely intent.
- Create and link a decision receipt before recording a human disposition.
- Treat acceptance as its own human-authority stage. Alignment, resolved
  assumptions or questions, and permission to continue do not imply
  acceptance.

## Git and lifecycle

- Require Git and the recorded dedicated or shared scope execution assignment
  for the current cycle. Preserve captured base ref/commit and branch role.
  Active work requires its recorded local ref. Serialize shared writes and
  preserve concurrent index additions and unrelated or unaccepted work.
- Do not amend, squash, rebase, force-push, merge, delete a branch, or deploy
  unless explicit authority and the stage prompt permit the specific action.
- Record only legal lifecycle transitions. Append transition evidence and
  preserve prior truthful claims when reopening or revising.
- Use the standard execution record for implementation and the standard
  delivery record for design or planning. Use standard completion evidence for
  every completed initiative. A status never substitutes for its required
  evidence.
- Follow exactly one delivery path for the declared type: implementation,
  design, or planning.

## Truthful completion and reporting

- Run the meaningful checks required for the exact bounded claim. Do not invent
  test, acceptance, release, production, or external-system evidence.
- Keep blockers, failed gates, unresolved questions, deferred work, and
  out-of-claim follow-up visible with an owner and re-entry condition.
- Reconcile every record the stage owns. Report exact paths changed, evidence
  used, verification performed, lifecycle effects, and remaining blockers.

## Scope and correlation recovery

- On correlation drift, stop the affected substantive stage and report the exact
  canonical row, reciprocal record, branch, or base mismatch. Recover missing
  documentary records only through the preceding procedure when sufficient
  immutable identity and actual owning-stage authority already exist.
- Return new-allocation errors to Stage 0 without reusing identity. Route a
  pre-effective record to its migration ledger. Never create a mapping merely
  to clear validation.
- Preserve recorded branch identity after authorized removal. An absent active
  branch or an absent terminal branch without linked removal evidence remains
  a blocker.



## Closure and immediate-parent integration

- Major branches start at the captured project integration base; minors start
  directly at their matching major; patches start directly at their matching
  minor; dedicated initiative branches start at their assigned scope. Capture
  actual parent tips before creation. No minor-from-minor or silent reparenting.
- Merge down means child into immediate parent: initiative -> assigned scope,
  patch -> minor, minor -> major, major -> project integration base. Work directly
  hosted on a scope records verified commits without self-merge or upward merge.
- A human close/finalize instruction for an identified governed target supplies
  its bounded local source-to-recorded-parent merge authority after every gate
  passes. Record it before acting; adoption, implementation and status alone
  supply no live merge authority. Pushes remain separately authorized.
- Scope closure is a separate operation in scope_closure.md. Closing one member
  never closes a sibling or scope. Capture membership/descendants, truthful
  closed dispositions, successful required child integration, aggregate checks,
  coherence/conflict reconciliation and closure authority before closing scope.
  Empty scopes need explicit no-work disposition. Closed parents require their
  own recorded reopening before new membership or integration.
- Never integrate a shared branch whole tip while it includes unfinished,
  unaccepted, cancelled or unrelated work. Keep integration blocked or use only
  separately authorized safe isolation/transfer preserving every owner. Closing
  one member does not authorize cherry-picking/reverting/discarding others.
- Inspect source/target tips and worktree, verify current target and source
  delta, run required checks, preserve failures, detect already integrated work,
  and record actual tips, authority, method/result commit and post-checks.
  Fast-forward and already integrated outcomes are recorded truthfully.
- Lifecycle closure and integration status remain separate. Failed integration
  preserves truthful prior completion and pending/blocked evidence. Retain
  branches unless their removal is separately authorized. New revision deltas
  require fresh evidence; historical merge receipts do not qualify new work.
- Pull requests are human-managed. Stages neither solicit nor prepare/create
  them, require a URL/hosting connection, or make local closure depend on one.
  Report external remote-policy constraints without bypassing them.
