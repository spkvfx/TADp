# Evidence

Preserve each source's identity, original claim, revision, and context. Binding
standing belongs to authoritative evidence; observations and proposals retain
their lower standing even when they are useful or recent. When information
changes, preserve the original and add a traceable revision or correction under
the applicable authority. Source preservation does not make every source an
invariant.

## Evidence classes

- [`authoritative`](authoritative/README.md) evidence is binding. Initiatives
  and products must meet and align to it.
- [`non-authoritative`](non-authoritative/README.md) evidence provides clues,
  context, and decision support. It cannot supersede authoritative evidence.

Cite a precise revision, heading, section, claim, or stable identifier whenever
evidence supports an initiative or product decision.

The authoritative Codex protocol suite is under
[`authoritative/LLM`](authoritative/LLM/00_Codex_Invariant_Suite_Index.md).
This is the single canonical authority hierarchy.

Begin with [specification 01](authoritative/LLM/01_Codex_Evidence_Authority_Specification.md)
when comparing sources. Cite a precise locator, classification, inspected
revision/date when available, and material limitations for each populated
claim. A realization and its suite remain permanently non-authoritative; an
accepted claim derived from them belongs in a separate accepted record.

Use the [coverage map](../template/TEMPLATE-COVERAGE.md) to select a source
template and destination for an evidence register, bounded evidence record,
architecture decision, rule, or realization catalog. Copy a reusable source
outside protected `template/` before populating it. Missing paperwork may be
recovered from sufficient actual evidence; it cannot supply a missing decision
or verification event. See [prerequisite recovery](../docs/guides/template-prerequisites-migration.md).

An invariant contradiction follows specification 01 and the
[invariant conflict register](authoritative/LLM/03_Codex_Invariant_Conflict_Register.md).
Incompatible initiative efforts also belong in the project
[work-conflict register](../initiatives/work-conflicts.md) under specification
10. Recording a conflict preserves the competing claims and their standing;
it does not resolve them.
