# Authoritative evidence

Artifacts in this directory are binding invariant evidence. They establish
conditions that initiatives and products must meet and align to.

The [`LLM`](LLM/00_Codex_Invariant_Suite_Index.md) directory contains the
binding Codex evidence-use, initiative-governance, lifecycle, Git, and scope
specifications. Codex and other agents must begin with its suite index.

- Preserve each artifact exactly. Do not edit, overwrite, or silently replace
  it unless explicitly instructed.
- Record every conflict with an initiative or product explicitly.
- Do not use a realization, proposal, product convenience, or
  `../non-authoritative` to weaken this evidence.
- When an authorized revision is required, retain the original and record the
  revision relationship, reason, and effective date.
