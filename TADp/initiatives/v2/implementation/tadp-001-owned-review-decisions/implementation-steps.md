## Delivery strategy

Apply the accepted implementation specification to candidate products only.
Keep one canonical owner per item and preserve the original review inventory.
Serialize shared contract/map/lock writes. Source identity, actual authority and
truthful check results are mandatory; no bypass or installed overwrite is a
recovery mechanism. The roadmap owns dependencies; source-map JSON owns exact
proposed paths and pinned provenance.

### Evidence / authoritative

revision and retains every other requirement. Proposed copies never replace

### Migration and rollback

- **Migration or backfill:** No live migration. Declare prospective adopter and
  source/destination consequences in the qualified manifest.
- **Mixed-version behavior:** Existing projects retain recorded governing
  revisions until their separately authorized adoption/migration.
- **Rollback:** Retained pinned source bytes plus exact candidate manifest and
  current comparison; preserve subsequent/unrelated changes. No destructive
  cleanup, history rewrite or installed lock refresh.

### Verification

Check complete prompt/rule/shape coherence, coverage declarations, IDs/links,
source protection and exclusion preservation. Inspect both ordinary and
failure behavior through the requirements below.

### Acceptance

No remaining clause negates the requested permission. No permission broadens
initiative accepted work, external target authority or lifecycle facts.
