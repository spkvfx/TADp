## Mapping rules

- Use exact source sections, revisions, standing and limits; the [proposal
  inventory](proposal/README.md) owns source precedence.
- Current normative directions are direct requested requirements or retained
  governing rules. No inferred assumption or independently answered product
  question exists to cite. That is why those IDs are absent; it is no acceptance.
- Before product execution link actual Stage 7 acceptance of this exact
  specification. New uncertainty gets its own stable source ID and ordinary
  decision gate, never an invented human answer.
- One map row owns each contract. Keep Proposed until actual source,
  specification, destination and verification evidence agree.
- Missing/conflicting authority blocks affected work. Preserve it in [open
  questions](open-questions.md) and applicable conflict records; do not weaken
  installed invariants or relabel a failed check as passing.
