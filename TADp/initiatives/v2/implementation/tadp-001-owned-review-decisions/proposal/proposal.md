## Executive proposal

Develop one reusable TADp behavior through the currently authorized candidate
development and qualification slice, so both numbered decision reviews permit
independent discovery of assumptions and open questions and properly authorized
dispositions at their canonical initiative or project owners. Initiative scope
continues to constrain the reviewed initiative's accepted work. It does not
prevent an authorized decision being recorded elsewhere within the actual
project and target authority.

The human is asked to accept this exact candidate-development claim after the
complete Proposed suite is coherent. Candidate output belongs in P/protocol;
this proposal grants no installed-library introduction, development completion,
promotion, rollout, integration, adoption, release or publication authority.
Successful qualification does not by itself permit ordinary `Completed` or
project development-completion claims. The installed development workflow's
subsequent exact D-introduction and actual completion requirements remain
future boundaries, requiring sufficient separate authority and evidence.

1. Both candidate review prompts explicitly support existing and new items of

## Proposed direction

### Product behavior or placement

Use the pinned installed reusable sources as provenance and change only their
declared C destinations. Retain copied-source protection and reconcile only
specifically covered candidate or isolated-fixture locks after coherent content
verification. Declare exact sources, destinations, triggers and owners in the
candidate coverage map before using the conditional project register. Keep one
canonical item owner and reuse existing receipt/index sources. Do not create
unused project registers by default or parallel decision sources.

### Domain and authority model

Every item has a stable ID, exact source text, originating review/evidence,
canonical owner/location, scope classification, dependencies, affected records
and current disposition. A human may answer outside original options or the
reviewed initiative, provided the answer can govern its actual target. Human
resolution remains the default, one ready item at a time. A recorded bounded
alternative method covers only its stated items after required authority receipt
and rule-specific exception; retain actual agent authorship, method, evidence,
result and limits. New IDs do not inherit delegation by proximity.

### System boundaries

Before an external record edit, identify exact target/current state, owner,
governing revision, applicable branch and sufficient write/decision authority.
When sufficient, record the item and authorized disposition without redundant
approval merely for external ownership. Otherwise preserve exact input and an
owned unresolved handoff while independent authorized work continues. Separate
scaffolds, repositories, forks and external services still require target-naming
authority. Recording a decision never implements its consequences or enlarges
the originating initiative's accepted work.

### Primary workflows

1. Inventory original review items and evidence, preserve dormant triggers and
   settled decisions, and append independently discovered assumptions or
   questions with exact provenance and canonical ownership.
2. Obtain each ready item's actual human disposition or apply only an already
   sufficiently authorized bounded method; preserve the receipt before source
   disposition changes and keep separate derived assumptions independently
   unresolved until their own exact disposition exists.
3. Reconcile only authorized affected records, link external dependencies and
   handoffs, and report complete coverage without claiming all external matters
   resolved. Applicable unresolved dependencies continue to block their actual
   handoff; initiative acceptance remains separate.

### Data and lifecycle

Preserve original texts, earlier options, dispositions, source IDs and receipt
links. Append replacements and supersession relationships instead of silently
rewriting history. Deduplicate repeated representations of an ID. Retain dormant
and deferred triggers and re-entry conditions; do not reopen a settled item
merely because a related matter is discovered. Closed initiative claims require
recorded authorized reopening before substantive refinement; changed accepted
claims require renewed acceptance, and independent outcomes route to Stage 0.

## Operational behavior

### Failure and recovery

Missing authority, ownership or evidence leaves the item unresolved with its
owned handoff. Invariant-conflicting answers retain exact conflict evidence and
route to the proper authority. Retries reuse sufficient receipts and stable IDs
without duplicate decisions or invented history. Qualification failures remain
failed evidence to repair or report. Recover candidate content through pinned
prior revisions and exact manifest comparisons, preserving later/unrelated
work; no automatic destructive rollback or history rewrite is proposed.

### Security and authorization

Project ownership follows the recorded current repository-owner role, not Git
author identity. Decision authority, write authority and resulting-work
authority remain distinct. No source link, shared Git root, role in another
fork, definition or outside-scope answer supplies a missing permission. Preserve
protected architecture/policy change control and all unexcepted gates.

### Observability

The canonical P initiative records retain actual commands, configured Python
environment, source revisions/digests, exact candidate paths, outputs, exit codes,
limitations, independent review findings and their dispositions. Reports
distinguish answered, unresolved, deferred, transferred and blocked items and
review coverage from acceptance, implementation and completion. No live-product
telemetry change is required for this document/workflow product.

## Compatibility and transition

The candidate is a proposed replacement, never the installed governing baseline.
Qualification assembles an isolated clean installation or workflow trial from
pinned clean sources plus exact candidate files and declared dependencies. It
must not depend on the live project, excluded populated history or example fork.
Prospective adoption, live migration, exact protected D introduction,
development completion, S promotion and mapped U rollout remain separate serial
boundaries. Preserve earlier compatible governing revisions, source identities,
decisions and accepted/closed claims until an authorized evidence-backed
transition. The qualified manifest must describe each downstream consumer and
remaining gate, including unchanged/inapplicable surfaces and recovery limits.

| Alternative | Benefit | Cost or reason not selected |

| Record all outside-initiative answers as context only | Matches current blanket restriction | Fails the expressly requested authorized external disposition behavior |

## Risks and tradeoffs

| Risk or tradeoff | Impact | Mitigation or evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery confused with resolution | Unauthorized answers could appear governing | Explicit unresolved creation, per-ID disposition and receipt checks |
| External decision confused with work authority | Scope or scaffold boundaries could expand silently | Identify separate owners/targets/permissions and test missing-authority handoffs |
| Incomplete consumer reconciliation | A remaining contract clause could negate the change | Compare both prompts, shared contract, specifications, shapes, coverage and navigation as one scoped diff |
| Validator overclaims semantic authority | Structural success might appear to approve a human decision | Optional checks validate record structure only; independent semantic review and actual authority remain required |
| Drift against earlier documentation candidates | Later delivery could overwrite proposed work | Preserve those drafts/history and compare exact navigation changes at each authorized boundary |

## Acceptance outcomes

- Both reviews preserve ordinary human resolution of existing in-scope items;
  independently define either kind of new item without a prior answer; allow
  each new item its own authorized same-review answer; and preserve/assess
  answers outside original options or reviewed initiative scope.
- Project-wide and other-initiative items reach authorized canonical owners
  without broadening the reviewed initiative. Missing external authority
  yields an owned unresolved handoff. Covered delegated answers retain actual
  agent authorship; uncovered new IDs remain unresolved.
- Derived assumptions retain independent dispositions. Dormant triggers,
  previously resolved items, stable IDs, exact prior texts/options and receipt
  history survive retries and deduplication.
- Invariant-conflicting answers, closed claims, changed accepted claims and
  independent outcomes retain conflict, reopening, renewed-acceptance and split
  requirements. External dependencies block only their applicable handoffs;
  reports distinguish unresolved, deferred and transferred work.
- Structural, relative-link and copied-source protection checks, focused
  regressions, a clean isolated installation/workflow trial and independent
  complete-diff review qualify exact candidate bytes. The manifest records
  compatibility, prospective adoption, migration, recovery, exclusions,
  dependencies, future source/consumer mapping, findings and remaining gates.
- No example-fork dependency, live-project review mutation, installed lock
  refresh, introduction, completion, promotion, rollout, integration, adoption,
  release or publication is claimed from candidate qualification.
