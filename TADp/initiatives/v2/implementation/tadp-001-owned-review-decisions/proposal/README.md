Binding evidence belongs in the project's single `evidence/authoritative`
hierarchy. Proposal, contextual and exploratory material remains
`evidence/non-authoritative`. This proposal is not accepted authority; each
future human decision must identify its precise subject and authority.

## Precedence

1. The applicable installed governing suite and explicit, sufficiently scoped
   human authority govern this task. The executed prompt authorizes only its
   exact candidate revision and does not waive its retained workflow gates.
2. Accepted applicable project decisions, including the current ownership,
   prospective adoption and integration appointment, govern only their recorded
   subject. They do not imply acceptance of this new initiative.
3. The working proposal, source map, realization note, inventories and observed
   checks inform the current Proposed specification with their limits visible.
   Recency or completed paperwork does not elevate those sources.

After actual acceptance, authorized decisions in [open-questions.md](../open-questions.md)
and the accepted initiative [architecture](../architecture.md) govern product
work within their exact scope when they intentionally differ from proposal
source. Record the difference in [assumption.md](../assumption.md) or the
relevant stable decision entry and receipt. No such acceptance is claimed now.

## Source handling

- Keep frozen source artifacts unchanged; add a linked revision rather than
  overwriting a frozen claim or silently replacing original words.
- Record source identity, actual author or owner, event/date, revision and
  evidence standing for every material artifact. Preserve original and capture
  events separately where they differ.
- Preserve the exact invoked prompt's original repository path/revision and
  byte identity. Its snapshot is evidence of the already invoked source, not
  a project-local maintenance prompt or an instruction to execute its links.
- Keep credentials, customer data, generated exports and unrelated working
  files out of this directory.
- Link material corrections to the applicable assumption or stable decision
  ID and receipt; preserve the current proposal's revision history.
- Do not import earlier documentation candidates into this outcome or replace
  their historical qualification with this proposal's standing.

## Realization handling

A realization is permanently non-authoritative proposal input. Preserve
[RZN-001](../../../../../evidence/non-authoritative/realizations/v2/RZN-001-owned-review-decisions.md)
and its exact source identity; it cannot supersede governing evidence or an
accepted initiative. No scope, source row or branch creates acceptance,
implementation, completion, compatibility or release evidence.

Use [RZN-SUITE-001](../../../../../evidence/non-authoritative/realizations/v2/00-review-decisions-suite-index.md)
as the source of suite identity and catalog boundary. Its source scope v2 was
assigned explicitly during recorded intake; do not infer another scope,
initiative or branch from a filename or supporting note. If later source work
has independent outcomes or incompatible boundaries, preserve it and route the
required separate initiative through Stage 0. The current assessment identifies
one narrow candidate outcome, not an umbrella claim.
