## Definition and disposition

Either numbered review may independently define either kind of item from human
input or evidence without a preceding answer, correction or existing source ID.
The human may define and answer a new item during that same review and may
answer outside the original options or reviewed initiative's scope. Codex may
discover unresolved items and clearly label a proposed recommendation. None of
that alone resolves an item or grants standing autonomous decision authority.

Definition begins unresolved. Preserve one canonical exact source text for each
stable ID, its origin, owner/location, scope, dependencies, affected records and
current disposition. Summaries link that section and deduplicate the same ID.
Preserve earlier options, words, dispositions and receipt links; append a
replacement/supersession relationship rather than silently rewriting history.

Human resolution is the default, one ready item at a time. Before a disposition
changes, capture exact human words, actual responder/authority and event in the
existing [receipt source](../initiatives/decision-receipts/decision-receipt.template.md),
instantiated at `decisions/<receipt-id>.md`. A specifically authorized alternative
method requires the authority receipt and rule-specific exception first;
attribute each covered result to its actual agent author, method, evidence and
limits. New IDs outside that coverage stay unresolved on the human path.

One response covers multiple IDs only when it explicitly and unambiguously
identifies the complete set and required outcomes. Answering a question does
not confirm a separate derived assumption: that item needs its own disposition,
which may be recorded during the same review. Do not reopen settled items only
because a related matter is discovered. Retain dormant/deferred re-entry triggers.

## Authority and external routing

Before an external record edit identify its exact target/current state, owner,
governing revision, applicable branch and sufficient write/decision authority.
When those suffice, define the item and record its actual authorized disposition
at that canonical destination without redundant approval solely for outside-
initiative ownership. When they do not, preserve exact input in a linked owned
unresolved handoff with proposed owner, blocker and re-entry condition. Continue
independent authorized review work without falsely reporting external resolution.

Another scaffold, repository, fork or external service requires authority naming
that target. A source link, shared Git root, role in one project or outside-scope
answer does not itself supply that authority. This register is not permission to
edit an external record or implement the decision's consequences.

## Coverage and dependent work

Report every original/new item, canonical owner, actual answer, unresolved item,
deferred trigger, transferred dependency and blocked handoff. Finishing the
bounded review does not require falsely resolving every external matter. An
unresolved external dependency still blocks its applicable initiative handoff;
it does not become an unrelated in-scope deliverable.

Recording a decision does not accept an initiative, implement resulting work,
revise protected architecture/policy or reopen a closed claim. Preserve exact
invariant conflicts and route their revision to the proper authority. Independent
outcomes route to Stage 0; coherent same-outcome revision retains its governing
route, changed accepted claims require renewed acceptance, and closed claims need
recorded authorized reopening before substantive refinement. Preserve frozen
proposals, prior claims, work-conflict and completion-feedback obligations.
