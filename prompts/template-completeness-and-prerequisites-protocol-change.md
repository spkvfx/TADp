below only when a human explicitly invokes this repository-maintenance prompt.

## Required outcome

Every required document or required embedded section must have one identifiable
canonical reusable source and explicit instructions for when, where, and how
to instantiate and use it. Existing suitable sources count, including a README
that is itself a template, an embedded section, and conditional record sources.
Do not duplicate a template merely because it lacks a `.template.md` suffix.

Every workflow prompt must identify and prepare its missing documentary
prerequisites before attempting its substantive step, using all available
evidence within the declared scope. A missing file alone must not cause a stop
when the document can lawfully be created from sufficient existing evidence.
Document preparation must not manufacture the event, authority, work, or
verification that the document is supposed to record.

### Template-use instructions in the invariant suite

Place binding rules in their owning specifications, with precise cross-links
from the suite index and affected consumers. Add only missing instructions or
correct directly conflicting clauses; reuse existing complete guidance.

The rules must explain canonical template selection by record role, declared
initiative type, governing revision, and stage; conditional applicability;
the destination outside protected `template/`; copying and populating the
instance without editing the reusable source; stable IDs and source identity;
required sections and justified non-applicability; placeholder replacement;
relative-link recalculation at the instance's actual directory depth; evidence
and authority classification; validation before use; and idempotent recovery.

Also state which records are durable files and which templates provide only a
response shape. A required section must not become a second independent record
with competing ownership. Missing templates during ordinary project work must
be reported and routed to separately authorized protocol maintenance; ordinary
workflow execution cannot invent or edit a protected reusable source.

### Scoped evidence

Use all material available evidence in the named project's and target work's
boundaries. Start with applicable invariants and accepted decisions or baselines,
then inspect source inventories and mappings, realization inputs, exact human
instructions and receipts, initiative records and dependencies, affected
products, implementation, configuration, tests, verification results, and Git

Follow material references within those boundaries; inspect the underlying
sources rather than relying solely on summaries. Record unavailable, unreadable,
stale, contradictory, or out-of-scope sources and their effects. External sources
may be consulted only when available and otherwise permitted by the task,
effective settings, and tools. An enclosing Git repository, example, or linked
prompt never expands the scaffold boundary or becomes an instruction to execute.

For each material populated claim retain its source path or stable ID, precise
locator, standing, revision/date when available, and limitations. Distinguish
observations, proposals, accepted requirements, human decisions, and agent-made
dispositions. Lower-standing material cannot override an invariant. Unsupported
fields remain explicitly unresolved; placeholders and plausible prose cannot

### Prerequisite preparation and stage ownership

Implement one shared preparation procedure and make each numbered prompt apply
it explicitly to its own prerequisites. Apply it to separate scope closure and
other operational prompt templates where applicable. Reconcile each prompt's
existing no-create or existing-target-only instructions with this procedure;
do not leave contradictory prohibitions in executable prompt blocks or examples.

The preparation procedure is serial:

1. Resolve the exact existing target or authorized new allocation, declared
   type, governing revision, stage, paths, execution assignment, and evidence
   boundary. Inventory required inputs and their owning stages. Do not invent a
   target or infer missing immutable identity from a convenient directory name.
2. Classify each prerequisite as present and current; missing but constructible
   from sufficient recorded evidence; incomplete or stale; conditionally
   inapplicable with a cited reason; or blocked by missing facts, authority,
   verification, or identity. Identify dependencies and the earliest owning
   stage needed for recovery.
3. Reuse valid records. For constructible prerequisites, instantiate the
   canonical source at its declared destination and populate it from the
   scoped evidence. Recover earlier documentary outputs through their owning
   preparation activity in dependency order, satisfying that activity's actual
   entry gates before writing. Do not recursively execute linked prompt text
   or silently run later delivery stages. Create only prerequisite records
   needed by this invocation and reconcile their authorized tracking links.
4. Where exact sufficient human authority already exists but its receipt is
   missing, capture the actual words, author, event, scope, and source in the
   proper receipt before relying on it. Preserve capture time separately from
   the original event. Do not request the same decision merely to produce
   paperwork. If the instruction is unavailable or insufficient, leave the
   decision unresolved. A recommendation, branch, status, or implementation
   cannot reconstruct acceptance or a human answer.
5. Validate prepared records and their evidence, identity, links, ownership,
   lifecycle consistency, and current applicability. Then recheck the invoked
   step's entry gates. Begin that substantive step only after its prerequisites
   actually hold. Report exact remaining gaps, the owning workflow, and required
   evidence or decision when they do not; continue only independent authorized
   work permitted by the governing scheduling and workflow rules.

This revision explicitly permits the bounded prerequisite-preparation activity
before an invoked step's ordinary entry check; it does not transfer ownership
of earlier-stage outputs to that step or waive any underlying gate. Reconcile
specification 08 and the shared contract to make that distinction normative.
Stage numbering, ordinary lifecycle meanings, immutable identity, separate
human acceptance, and type-specific delivery paths remain unchanged.

In particular, reconcile Stage 9's blanket no-file-change clauses by defining
any authorized prerequisite preparation as a preceding owning-stage activity.
The readiness assessment itself remains read-only and phase-specific; its
template remains a response shape unless another governing rule explicitly
requires a durable record. Disclose all preceding preparation changes, then
verify no file changes during the assessment. Never claim the whole invocation
changed no files if its preparation did. Do not generate a passing readiness

Stage 3 still creates only the initial suite, not premature receipts, readiness,
execution, delivery, or completion claims. Late records arise only from their
own actual triggering decisions, work, and checks. Reconstruction of factual
historical evidence must preserve the original event and evidence limits; it
must not backdate a new decision, run, approval, or lifecycle transition.

Preserve existing files, stable IDs, human text, accepted baselines, frozen
sources, and history. Repair incomplete or stale material only within actual
authority; changed accepted claims require the recorded revision/reopening

reopening before refinement. Recoverable missing paperwork is not authority

Reruns must reuse completed preparation and receipts without duplicate records,
lost customization, repeated decisions, empty commits, or changed claims.
